# MoneyMate v1.5.0 - The Work Update

This release introduces the highly requested **Work Tab** for tracking professional jobs, customer payments, and business analysis.

## What's New?

### 💼 New Work Tab
*   **Customer Job Tracking**: Log jobs like PAN Card, Aadhar Card, Passport applications, and more.
*   **Payment Status Management**: Track whether a job is **Paid**, **Unpaid**, or **Undecided**.
*   **Dynamic Entry Form**: Automatically adapts based on payment status.
*   **Photo Capture**: Attach photos of IDs or documents directly to work records. Photos are compressed and stored securely within the app.

### 📊 Business Analysis
*   **Earning Dashboard**: See today's earnings at a glance.
*   **Job Stats**: Track total jobs, paid vs. unpaid counts.
*   **Work Analysis**: Visual breakdown of which jobs you perform most frequently.

### ⚙️ Enhanced Settings
*   **Work Name Manager**: Customize your list of job types (e.g., add "Visa", "License", etc.).
*   **Version Info**: Updated to v1.5.0.

## Improvements & Fixes
*   Updated app description to reflect new business tracking capabilities.
*   Optimized image compression for faster performance and lower storage usage.
*   Enhanced UI with gold accents for the Work section.
