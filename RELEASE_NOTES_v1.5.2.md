# MoneyMate v1.5.2 - Unpaid Tracking & UI Polish

This release enhances the Work Tracker with better visibility into pending payments and job statistics.

## What's New?

### 💰 Unpaid Amount Tracking
*   **Pending Totals**: The Work Tracker dashboard now shows the total amount of money pending from all unpaid jobs.
*   **Clear Overview**: Easily see exactly how much you are owed at a glance.

### 🔢 Enhanced Job Statistics
*   **Descriptive Labels**: Job counts now include the word "work" (e.g., "3 work") for better clarity.
*   **Detailed Unpaid Stats**: The unpaid section now shows both the total amount and the number of pending jobs in a compact, readable format.

## Other Improvements
*   Updated versioning to v1.5.2.
*   Refined layout spacing in the stats card for better mobile readability.
